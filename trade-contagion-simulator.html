Trade Contagion

Cut buying power in one or more economies and watch the demand shock propagate through export links, round by round, until the network settles. Amplification, collapse threshold, and per-country shocks are all live.

Model limitations. Demand-side only — no supply cutoffs and no financial or currency contagion, so real outcomes would be worse. Damage can exceed 100% of GDP and is a relative stress score, not literal GDP loss (the world-GDP aggregate caps each economy at 100%). Dependency shares are approximate 2024–25 figures; the Philippines' US share (30%) is intentionally set above its goods-only figure to capture BPO revenue and remittances. No recovery dynamics; ASEAN and the EU are treated as single blocs.
