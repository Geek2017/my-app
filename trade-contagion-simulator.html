Trade Contagion

Shock any economy — cut its buying power, choke its export supply, crash its currency — and watch the damage propagate through trade links, remittances, and commodity markets, round by round. Debt-to-GDP drives each country's credit fragility and fiscal room; FX reserves raise its collapse resistance. Open a card's levers for the full kit.

Model limitations. Damage can exceed 100% of GDP and is a relative stress score, not literal GDP loss (the world-GDP aggregate caps each economy at 100%). Dependency shares, debt ratios, reserve buffers, remittance and commodity exposures are approximate 2024–25 figures; the Philippines' US export share (30%) is intentionally set above its goods-only figure to capture BPO revenue. Supply criticality (Taiwan chips, Gulf oil) is a stylized leverage factor, not measured input-output data. Financial and currency contagion between third countries is still absent. No recovery dynamics; ASEAN and the EU are treated as single blocs.
